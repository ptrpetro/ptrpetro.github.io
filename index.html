About Me

I moved to the U.S. from Hungary with a backpack and not much of a plan beyond figuring it out as I went - so when I got to college and could barely get a for-loop to compile, that felt familiar territory. Since then I've shipped a production website a real business depends on, trained a detection model on messy real-world video, and learned that most of engineering happens after the first version already "works."

What I care about now is the gap between a class project and something someone else actually relies on - the edge cases, the client who changes their mind, the deploy that breaks a thing you never touched. That gap is where I've been spending my time, and it's where I want to keep working.

I was also one of a small group selected for Entrepreneurship for Defense, working alongside UCF research faculty and U.S. Navy stakeholders on adaptive optics for defense and telecom - my first real look at engineering outside a classroom.

Projects

01
AI Parking Occupancy Detection
Problem
Real lots aren't a neat grid, and nobody knows what's actually open until they've already driven the row.
Solution
A Flask app that runs live video through YOLOv11, maps detections onto custom-defined zones for irregular layouts, and feeds occupancy back to a lightweight frontend in real time.
PythonFlaskYOLOv11OpenCVJavaScript
The messiest part wasn't the detection model - it was defining zones that actually matched the lot's real geometry instead of a clean grid. I ended up building a small zone-annotation tool just to make that mapping fast to iterate on.

Problem
Pomodoro timers reset the second you close the laptop, and nobody learns anything from a session once it's over.
